Round by round · the story

Six races. One season.

Scroll through the season — one round at a time. Each panel is a chapter: the city, the result, and what made it the round it was.

Round 01
April 2025 · Tamil Nadu

Chennai

DNS · pre-season commitment

Missed the season opener. The campaign would start fresh in Coimbatore in June — and inside one round, deliver a debut podium. Sometimes the best chapter is the one that didn't happen.

Round 02
June 2025 · Tamil Nadu

Coimbatore

P2 JINRC · P4 INRC3 · Both legs P2 · Debut podium

First INRC start. Trading times with India's most experienced rally crews — and walking off the stage with a P2 in JINRC, P4 overall in INRC3. The whole season turned on this weekend.

Round 03
September 2025 · Karnataka

Coorg

P3 overall · L1: P3 · L2: P2

Forest twisties, plantation roads, narrow lines. Closed Leg 2 in P2 to finish on the overall podium — a result earned in exactly the kind of stages that separate drivers from passengers.

Round 04
December 2025 · Bangalore Rally

Bangalore

P1 Leg 1 · P2 overall · First INRC win

First stage win, on the flagship round. Took Leg 1 outright; held P2 across the weekend. The week the season turned from "promising debutant" to "real contender".

Round 05
January 2026 · Madhya Pradesh

Indore

Won both legs · 2× wins · Most dominant round

Different car. Different line. Different driver. Won both legs back-to-back on Madhya Pradesh gravel — the most dominant single-round performance of the season.

Finale 06
February 2026 · Maharashtra

Nashik

Rear beam failure L1 · P3 L2 · Sealed runner-up

Mechanical failure on Leg 1. Rebuilt overnight, returned Leg 2, brought it home in P3 — salvaging enough points to seal the JINRC runner-up trophy. Earned the hard way.
